🎓 EduPredict AI
Student Performance Prediction & Risk Intelligence Platform
EduPredict AI is an intelligent educational analytics platform designed to help educators identify students who may be at academic risk and take proactive action before performance declines further.

The platform combines Machine Learning, academic analytics, risk prediction, data visualization, and personalized recommendations to transform student data into actionable insights.

📌 Problem Statement
Traditional academic evaluation often identifies struggling students only after their grades have significantly declined.

EduPredict AI addresses this problem by analyzing student-related factors and providing early insights into:

Academic performance
Attendance and engagement
Performance trends
Academic risk levels
Factors influencing predictions
Recommended interventions
The goal is to enable educators to move from reactive academic monitoring to proactive intervention.

✨ Key Features
🤖 AI-Powered Performance Prediction
The system analyzes student information and uses machine-learning techniques to estimate academic performance and identify potential risks.

⚠️ Risk Classification
Students can be categorized according to their predicted academic risk, such as:

🟢 Low Risk
🟡 Medium Risk
🔴 High Risk
This allows educators to prioritize students who require additional attention.

📊 Analytics Dashboard
The platform provides visual insights into student performance through dashboards and charts, making large amounts of academic data easier to understand.

🔍 Performance Analysis
EduPredict AI can analyze important academic indicators such as:

Attendance
Academic scores
Assignments/tasks
Examination performance
Performance trends
Other relevant student factors
💡 Personalized Recommendations
Instead of simply predicting a student's performance, the system can provide actionable suggestions that educators can use to support students.

Possible interventions include:

Additional academic support
Remedial learning
Mentoring
Tutoring
Increased monitoring
Structured study/review sessions
🧠 Explainable Insights
The platform is designed to help educators understand why a student may be classified as at risk rather than presenting a prediction as a black box.

🔄 What-If / Simulation Analysis
Simulation capabilities can be used to explore how changes in relevant student factors could affect predicted outcomes, helping educators evaluate possible intervention
